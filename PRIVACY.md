# Privacy Policy — Christmas Together 2026

**Effective date:** October 9, 2026<br>
**App:** Christmas Together 2026 (Android, package `ph.christmastogether.app`)<br>
**Developer:** Mplaystore

Christmas Together is built so that your Christmas stays yours. You don't create an account, and we don't run ads, analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you add to the app is stored **only on your device**:

- your gift list (including inaanak and cash gifts) and gift status
- your budget and spending entries
- your plans, saved favorites, calendar, reunions and parties
- your "My Christmas 2026" activities and notes
- photos you choose to add

We cannot see any of this, and none of it is uploaded to us or anyone else.

**Photos.** You pick photos yourself from your phone. The app makes a smaller copy, which also removes hidden data such as where the photo was taken. The copy is kept only inside the app.

## Location

If you tap **Near me**, the app asks for your location once to sort places by distance. It is used on the spot, never saved, and never sent anywhere. If you don't allow it, you can pick your city instead.

## What the app sends over the internet

- **Christmas listings.** The app may download the public list of Christmas places and events. This sends no personal information.
- **Suggestions.** If you use **Suggest a place**, only what you type in that form (place name, city, description and an optional link) is sent to us so we can check it before listing it. Don't include personal details there.
- **Fonts.** The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google receives your device's IP address. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Links.** Listings link to outside websites (for example, a mall or venue page). These open in your browser and follow that website's own privacy policy.

## Sharing

When you share a greeting card or plan, the app opens your phone's normal share menu. Nothing is sent until you choose an app and send it yourself.

## What we don't do

We don't collect your name, contacts, phone number or email. We don't sell or share data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings and privacy › Delete all my data** to erase everything from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes all of it.

## Children

The app is made for families and is not directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

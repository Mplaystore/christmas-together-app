FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Christmas Together 2026 helps Filipino families plan a happier, less stressful Pasko — all in one app, with everything kept private on your phone.

FIND CHRISTMAS PLACES AND EVENTS
• Browse Christmas lights, events, food spots, staycations, gift shopping and decorations
• Tap Near me or pick your city to see what's close
• Save favorites and turn them into plans with a date and time
• Know a great Christmas village or light show? Suggest it and we'll check it before it's listed

PLAN EVERY GIFT
• Keep one gift list for family, loved ones, friends, office mates and your inaanak
• Track cash gifts too: to prepare, cash ready, in envelope, given
• See at a glance what's still to buy, bought, wrapped and given

STAY ON BUDGET
• Set a budget for gifts, food, decorations and more
• Log your spending and see right away when you go over

YOUR CHRISTMAS CALENDAR
• Add Simbang Gabi, Noche Buena, reunions and office parties
• Keep all your reunions and parties in one place

SEND A GREETING
• Make your own Christmas card with a family photo and message
• "Christmas Across the Miles" cards for loved ones working abroad
• Share through Messenger, Viber, email or any app

REMEMBER YOUR CHRISTMAS
• Record the places you visited and the parties you attended, with photos
• Keep your whole Christmas 2026 in one place to look back on

Plus a countdown to Christmas, festive music, and the stories behind Filipino Christmas decorations like the parol.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your gifts, budget, plans and photos stay on your phone. You can delete everything anytime in Settings.

Maligayang Pasko!
